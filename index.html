About Vertical Farming

Vertical Farming is a modern agricultural technique in which crops are grown in vertically stacked layers. It helps to save space, reduce water consumption and produce fresh vegetables throughout the year. This technology is useful in cities where farming space is limited.

Vertical Farming Methods

Hydroponics

Plants grow without soil using nutrient-rich water.

Aeroponics

Plant roots receive nutrients through a fine mist.

Aquaponics

A system that combines fish farming with plant growing.

Benefits of Vertical Farming

💧 Save Water

Uses less water than many traditional farming methods.

🌱 Less Space

Grow more crops in a smaller area.

🥬 Fresh Food

Fresh vegetables can be produced near consumers.

🌍 Eco Friendly

Supports efficient and sustainable agriculture.
